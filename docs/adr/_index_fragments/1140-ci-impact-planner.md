| [ADR-1140](1140-ci-impact-planner.md) | Route required CI work by measured impact instead of pre-declared path filters. Ports `lusoris/k8s` #4964: every workflow hosting an aggregator-required check loses its workflow-level `paths:`/`paths-ignore:` filter and always starts; inside each required job a fail-closed planner (`scripts/ci/plan-ci-impact.py`, driven by `.github/ci-impact.json`) diffs the event's exact revisions (merge-base aware for PRs, exact `before..head` for pushes, NUL-safe name-status with renames) and maps paths onto selectors (`c_core`, `python`, `ai`, `go`, `rust`, `docs`, `shell`, `actions`, `container`) plus closures (`golden_harness`, `tiny_ai`, `python_lint`); heavy steps run only when their selector is impacted, otherwise the job emits a deterministic notice and reports success with the reason. Anything unprovable — unknown top-level path, delete/rename/copy, a CI-authority file, missing merge-base, non-linear push, over-large diff — collapses to `mode=full` (today's behaviour). A doc- or `renovate.json`-only PR now runs the sub-minute gates instead of ~25 min of matrix time; the three divergent `paths-ignore` copies are gone; `scripts/ci/tests/test_ci_impact.py` fails if a top-level path is missing from the map or a required-context workflow regains a path filter. ADR-0313's absent-means-pass rule stays as a backstop only. | Accepted | ci, build, docs, fork-local |
