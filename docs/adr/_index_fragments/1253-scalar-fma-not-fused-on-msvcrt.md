| [ADR-1253](1253-scalar-fma-not-fused-on-msvcrt.md) | Scalar references compute their fused multiply-add themselves rather than trusting libm. `fmaf()` is a genuine FMA on glibc, musl and the UCRT but not on the legacy `msvcrt.dll` that MSYS2's MINGW64 links, so the scalar path rounded twice where its SIMD twin rounded once and ADR-1207's gate measured `ssimulacra2` 0.37 points apart on the `Windows MinGW64` lane. `vmaf_fmaf_exact()` evaluates in `double` and rounds once, which is the correctly-rounded fused result on every host — verified bit-identical to glibc `fmaf` and to `vfmadd213ss` over 20 million triples. | Accepted | simd, build, windows |
