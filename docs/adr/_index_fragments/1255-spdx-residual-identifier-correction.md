PR #1457 fixes 935 of them as a side effect of the EUPL-1.2 relicensing; its provenance vetoes leave a residual of 92 (docs, `deploy/` and `config/` YAML, skill templates, `ai/` scripts, Dockerfile OCI labels) plus one `BSD+Patent`. The residual is corrected in the rc.1 train to the identifier `LICENSE` and 367 other files in this repo already use, so rc.1's SPDX validity does not depend on a breaking change still under review.
