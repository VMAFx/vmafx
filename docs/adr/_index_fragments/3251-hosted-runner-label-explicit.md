| [ADR-3251](3251-hosted-runner-label-explicit.md) | Every job on a GitHub-hosted Linux runner names its image by version (`ubuntu-26.04`, `ubuntu-26.04-arm`); `ubuntu-latest`, its larger-runner forms and `ubuntu-slim` are refused by `scripts/ci/check_hosted_runner_labels.py` (pre-commit hook, third step of `make lint-actions`). An older versioned image needs a compatibility target named in the script (`verify-native-artifacts` on `ubuntu-24.04`, ADR-1354) or a dated fallback in the declared exception list that ends within 90 days. Supersedes ADR-0802 | Accepted | ci, build |
