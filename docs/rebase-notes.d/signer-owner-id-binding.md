## Tiny-model signer bound to VMAFx's owner ID (2026-10-09)

- `core/src/dnn/model_loader.c::vmaf_dnn_verify_signature()` reads the bundle
  once (`slurp_json_file()`), checks it with
  `core/src/dnn/signer_identity.c::vmaf_dnn_signer_check_bundle()` and passes
  cosign a `mkstemp(3)` copy of the checked bytes, with
  `VMAF_DNN_SIGNER_IDENTITY_REGEXP` and `VMAF_DNN_SIGNER_OIDC_ISSUER` from
  `signer_identity.h`. **On sync**: model verification is fork-only (ADR-0211);
  keep the check before cosign, the private copy, and the anchored expression.
  `signer_identity.c` is in `dnn_sources` and in every test or fuzz target that
  compiles `model_loader.c` directly.
- When the organisation is renamed, the login in
  `VMAF_DNN_SIGNER_IDENTITY_REGEXP`, `vmaf_dnn_signer_identity_allowed()` and
  the guides changes; `VMAF_DNN_SIGNER_OWNER_ID` does not.
- `core/test/dnn/signer_fixtures.h` is generated by
  `core/test/dnn/gen_signer_fixtures.py` from a release bundle; do not edit it.
