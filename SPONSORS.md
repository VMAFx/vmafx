# Sponsors

You can sponsor VMAFx from $5 a month. Sponsors are thanked here by tier;
nothing else changes: no paid support, no feature voting, no paywalls.
Everything stays open source.

Sponsorship pays for CI time, cloud GPU test time on hardware the project does
not own, and, as the next goal, an AI workstation. The first goal is $250 a
month. The page [Support VMAFx](docs/support-vmafx.md) explains what the money
buys.

## How to sponsor

- **GitHub Sponsors (USD):** <https://github.com/sponsors/lusoris>. The
  profile belongs to the maintainer's personal account and covers VMAFx and
  the maintainer's related open-source work. GitHub accepts US dollars only.
- **Ko-fi (EUR):** <https://ko-fi.com/lusoris>.
- **Patreon (EUR):** <https://www.patreon.com/Lusoris>. The same four tiers
  in euros; GitHub Sponsors also shows them ("pay through Patreon").

Custom amounts are allowed on GitHub Sponsors.

## Recognition rules

- **Staying anonymous.** Tell the maintainer in your sponsorship message or in
  a discussion at <https://github.com/VMAFx/vmafx/discussions>, and nothing is
  listed for you. Nobody is listed without being a sponsor.
- **Logos.** Company and Lead logos are placed by the maintainer after you
  send them. Send an SVG or a transparent PNG; the maintainer checks that the
  logo is yours to use, sizes it to the same height as the others, and links it
  to the address you give. The Lead logo comes first and is slightly larger.
- **Links.** Links are plain links to your own site or profile. The maintainer
  can decline a logo or link that is unlawful, misleading or unrelated to a
  business, and then lists nothing.
- **Ending a sponsorship.** When a monthly sponsorship ends, the entry is
  removed in the next update of this file.
- **No influence.** Sponsoring does not buy a say in the roadmap, early
  access, support or a changed licence.

Contact is through GitHub: an issue or a discussion on
[VMAFx/vmafx](https://github.com/VMAFx/vmafx).
