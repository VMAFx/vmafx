- **Vulkan integer compute backend (draft, ADR-3401).** `-Denable_vulkan=true` builds a fp64-free Vulkan compute path behind the dynamic loader (`vulkan-1` / `libvulkan.so.1`, VK_NO_PROTOTYPES, no SDK linkage, no volk, no VMA) with its first consumer: the `adm_vulkan` twin of integer ADM, bit-exact against the CPU twin on `shaderInt64`-capable devices (RTX 5080, RTX 4060 Laptop and Intel Raptor Lake iGPU validated). CLI: `--backend vulkan`, `--vulkan_device`, `--no_vulkan`; `vmaf --list-backends` and `libvmaf_vulkan.h` (`vmaf_vulkan_state_init` / `vmaf_vulkan_import_state` / `vmaf_vulkan_state_free` / `vmaf_vulkan_available`) follow the HIP surface. Disabled by default: every entry point returns `-ENOSYS` (#3319).
