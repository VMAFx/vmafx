- **Assert the Docker image licence labels against the manifest's licence-bearing components** ([#3079](https://github.com/VMAFx/vmafx/issues/3079)). The check resolves `from` references, compares each published stage's effective label with the component union (including fixed components such as zimg's WTFPL), keeps the non-SPDX NVIDIA EULA text out of the label, reads labels split across Dockerfile continuation lines, compares every stage with its own manifest record (the server, ROCm and oneAPI stages differ from the CLI and CUDA records), skips fixture trees that do not carry production artifacts, and rejects missing, extra or lowercase labels.
